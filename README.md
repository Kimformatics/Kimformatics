## Professional and Academic Background 🎓
I'm a Biomedical Informatcist, Data Scientist, Educator, and Health Disparities Researcher with more than 25 years of Scientific Research experience and over a decade working in Data Science and Informatics. My interdisciplinary training includes a PhD in Biomedical Informatics, master's degrees in Chemistry and Pharmacology, and I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Engineering (MSE) degree in Environmental Engineering and Science at Johns Hopkins University.

I currently serve as a Postdoctoral Fellow at Howard University, where my research sits at the intersection of Environmental Health, Environmental Justice, Data Science, and Health Equity. My work focuses on understanding how Environmental Exposures, including air pollution and other environmental hazards contribute to maternal health and Health Disparities in historically underserved populations in Washington, DC. By integrating environmental justice screening (EJScreen) metrics, air quality monitoring data, CDC PLACES, the Social Vulnerability Index (SVI), Census Data, and other Population Health datasets, I develop Data-Driven approaches to identify communities disproportionately impacted by Environmental Risk Factors and translate those findings into actionable public health insights.

I'm also the Founder and Principal Informatics Scientist of [AIAD Consulting](https://aiad-consulting-june2026.netlify.app/), where I lead projects spanning Biomedical Informatics, Healthcare Informatics, Environmental Informatics, Clinical Data Science, and Artificial Intelligence. I also develop workforce development programs like the [Public Health Data Analytics Accelerator](PHDAA)](https://aiad-public-health-data-accelerator.netlify.app/) that prepare students and professionals for careers in Data Analytics by teaching practical, job-ready skills in Microsoft Excel, SQL, R, Python, Artificial Intelligence, Data Visualization, and Dashboard Development.
